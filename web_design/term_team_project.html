Term Project vs. Team Project

While they sound almost identical, Term Project defines when and how long a project runs, whereas Team Project defines who works on it.

Feature Term Project Team Project
Primary Focus Timeframe & Scope
Spans an academic term/semester		 Collaboration & Roles
Group execution & teamwork
Structure Milestones spread across weeks (e.g., proposal, midterm check-in, final presentation) Division of labor, peer evaluations, and shared accountability
Participants Can be individual OR group-based Must involve two or more people
Goal Demonstrate comprehensive mastery of course material over time Build soft skills, manage group dynamics, and execute larger-scale tasks

Term Project Solo

  • Spans an entire semester
  • Completed independently by one person
  • Example: Writing an individual 20-page research thesis due at the end of the term.

Short-Term Team Project

  • Focuses on immediate group collaboration
  • Completed in a short timeframe
  • Example: A 2-day hackathon or a 1-week group workshop assignment.
